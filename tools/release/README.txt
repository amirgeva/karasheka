KARASHEKA {{VERSION}}
Retro karate in the apartment.

HOW TO PLAY
Run karasheka.exe. Nothing needs to be installed.

Two karateka fight it out across the living room, the kitchen, the bedroom
and the study. Chairs, tables, the sofa and the fridge are fair game: jump
onto them to dodge, and dive kick from up there for extra damage. Light
furniture breaks under a heavy landing.

In the menu, play against the computer (Easy, Normal or Hard), against a
friend on the same keyboard, or just watch two computers fight.

KEYS
Each fighter uses nine keys laid out like a numeric keypad, plus a strike
key and a guard key:

             Q W E        Y U I        7 8 9
             A S D        H J K        4 5 6
             Z X C        N M ,        1 2 3
  Strike:    Left Shift   Space        Right Shift (or Num 0)
  Guard:     Left Ctrl    L            Right Ctrl (or Num Enter)

Playing alone you can mix them, e.g. the numpad with Left Shift / Left Ctrl.

On their own:           jump back / jump up / jump forward
                        walk back / mid guard / walk forward
                        roll back / crouch / roll forward

With the strike key:    backflip kick / uppercut / flying kick
                        roundhouse / punch / front kick
                        low punch / low kick / sweep

With the guard key:     up = high guard, nothing = mid, down = low

(Shown for the fighter on the left; back and forward mirror on the right.)
In the air, strike is an air kick and down + strike a dive kick.
Each attack is blocked only by the guard at its height: high for the
roundhouse, the uppercut and anything from the air; mid for punches and the
front kick; low for the low kick and the sweep. Every attack takes at least
200 ms to land, so you can see it coming. Keys meant together may be pressed
in either order, a moment apart.

  Esc          Pause
  F1           Show all keys
  F2           Sound on / off
  F11          Fullscreen

Your menu choices are saved in %APPDATA%\Karasheka\Karasheka\settings.ini.
