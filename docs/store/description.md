Two karateka square off in a small apartment, and nobody moved the furniture
first. Trade punches and kicks across the living room, then jump onto the
coffee table to get out of the way, or onto the kitchen counter, the bed or
the desk. From up there, a dive kick comes down hard, and the higher you
dive from, the more it hurts.

Every attack lands high, mid or low, and only the guard at that height
stops it. Every attack also takes at least a fifth of a second to land, so
you can watch the wind-up, read it and pick the right guard in time. Guard
the head and you get swept off your feet. Guard low and you eat a
roundhouse. Get knocked down onto a chair and it breaks under you.

The controls are in the spirit of the one-button karate games of the 1980s:
nine direction keys laid out like a numeric keypad, one strike key and one
guard key.

**Features**

- 1 player against the computer, or 2 players on one keyboard
- Three computer levels: Easy (beatable by a beginner), Normal and Hard
- A watch mode: two computers fight while you look on
- Four rooms: living room, kitchen, bedroom and study, each with its own
  furniture to climb on and fight around
- Breakable furniture: chairs, stools, tables, the TV and more give way
  under a heavy landing, taking whatever stands on them along
- Matches of 1 round, best of 3 or best of 5; 30, 60 or 99 seconds a round,
  or no time limit
- Tour the flat (a new room every round) or stay in one room
- Keys pressed a moment apart still count as pressed together, in either
  order
- Pixel-art graphics, fullscreen or windowed; your menu settings are
  remembered

**Moves**

With the strike key:

- Punch, front kick, roundhouse to the head
- Low punch, low kick, sweep (knocks you down)
- Uppercut (knocks a jumper out of the air), flying kick, backflip kick
- In the air: air kick, or a dive kick straight down

On their own the keys walk, jump (straight up, forward or back), crouch and
roll, and a roll takes you under high attacks and right past your opponent.

**Guarding**

- High (guard + up): roundhouse, uppercut, flying kick and anything from the
  air or from above
- Mid (guard alone, or the middle key): punches and the front kick
- Low (guard + down): low kick and sweep

Crouching on its own blocks nothing, but high attacks pass over you.

**Winning**

Knock your opponent out, or have more health left when time runs out. Win
the most rounds to take the match.

**Controls**

Keyboard only. Each player uses nine direction keys plus strike and guard.
Playing alone you can mix the layouts, for example the numpad with Left
Shift and Left Ctrl.

- Left hand: Q W E / A S D / Z X C, strike Left Shift, guard Left Ctrl
- Right hand: Y U I / H J K / N M , (comma), strike Space, guard L
- Numpad: 7 8 9 / 4 5 6 / 1 2 3, strike Right Shift (or Num 0), guard Right
  Ctrl (or Num Enter)
- Esc: pause
- F1: show all the keys
- F2: sound on / off
- F11 or Alt+Enter: fullscreen

Windows only. Two players share one keyboard; there is no online play.
